# EX-02-Cross-Platform-Prompting-Evaluating-Diverse-Techniques-in-AI-Powered-Text-Summarization

## AIM
To evaluate and compare the effectiveness of prompting techniques (zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, role-based) across different AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) in a specific task: text summarization.

## Scenario:
Final Prompt:

Step 1:
Summarize the following 500-word article on “The Basics of Blockchain Technology” in 120–150 words for undergraduate students. Keep it clear and beginner-friendly.

Step 2 (Feedback):
Please revise the summary to make it even simpler and include at least two real-world examples of blockchain applications.

Additionally, create a mind map of the key concepts and generate relevant images that visually explain blockchain technology and its applications.

## Algorithm

## Result
Step 1:
Summary
Blockchain technology is a decentralized, digital ledger that records transactions across a network of computers. It uses cryptography to secure and validate transactions, making it a secure and transparent way to conduct transactions. The blockchain is maintained by a network of nodes, rather than a single central authority, which makes it resistant to tampering and censorship. Each block in the chain contains a number of transactions, and once a block is added to the chain, the information it contains cannot be altered. This technology has many potential applications, including cryptocurrency, supply chain management, and voting systems. Blockchain technology allows for secure, transparent, and efficient data management, making it an attractive solution for various industries.

Step 2:
Revised Summary with Examples and Mind Map
Revised Summary
Blockchain is a digital ledger that records transactions across a network of computers. It's secure, transparent, and decentralized, making it ideal for various applications. For example, Walmart uses blockchain to track food products, ensuring safety and authenticity. Another example is Maersk, which uses blockchain to streamline supply chain management and reduce costs. Blockchain technology has the potential to transform industries by providing a secure and efficient way to conduct transactions and manage data.
